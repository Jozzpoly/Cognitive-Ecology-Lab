## Why this was investigated

R0's first sweep (run [37967542316](https://github.com/Jozzpoly/Cognitive-Ecology-Lab/actions/runs/37967542316)) made authored episodic recall beat both habit and a cheap cooldown in **all 640** comparisons (128 seeds × 5 scripted perturbation schedules) under its sole east-rich ecology. This was suspicious: an asymmetric, fixed two-source environment could encode an almost predetermined benefit from avoiding the western source.

Instead of praising universal success, an explicit counterworld campaign varied **world conditions**, while holding the actor-private data contract and three policy implementations fixed.

## Findings / nonclaims

1. **Causal utility is conditional.** In one hand-authored world, a richer episodic scoring rule dominates; in the inverted world, simple cooldown often beats it.
2. **No-need ablation is important.** In abundant conditions there are still recall consultations, but no observed difference in actor trajectories or deliveries versus habit in targeted tests. The extra reasoning has no measured material lift.
3. **No universal winner was established.** Results depend strongly on stocking rates and initial conditions, with extreme low-dimensional profile structure.
4. **Not a significance claim.** The 128 seeds vary narrow replenishment phases, not independent world geometries, agents, economics or rewards. The five intervention schedules are hand-authored and overlapping; paired rows are strongly related.
5. **Consultations are calls to a deterministic synchronous helper.** They are not measured Jev API latency, model cost, learned memory retrieval, or proof of a second independent intelligence. Real world/advisor temporal handoff and failure modes remain open.
6. **Fairness caution:** the cooldown reference also uses historical empty observations; its difference from recall is a concrete authored scoring policy and amount/type of history used, not an absolute split between memory and no memory.
7. **Owner experience remains UNKNOWN.** CI and headless Chromium do not validate usefulness, readability, or surprise.

## Decision / next question

The R0 first apparatus is qualified only to **show falsifying A/B contrasts**. Do not promote its toy courier, resource rates, static two-site map, recall score, or locally synchronous call structure into cognitive architecture.

Next useful question: Does a **separately operating, interruptible cognitive process** provide value once advice can be delayed, stale, refused, costly, contradicted or ignored? Test against a simple cooldown and zero-advice ablations, preserving World truth and actor-private evidence. The first richer mechanism should not silently gain motor authority.

Owner should be able to explore the ecological inversion directly in the browser, not read CI logs to see the whole finding.
